# ⚡ APEX FIT MERN — Athletic White & Crimson Edition

> **Elite Full-Stack Fitness & Workout Application**  
> Built with MongoDB, Express.js, React.js (Vite), Node.js, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and the Web Audio API for immersive sound effects.

---

## 🌟 Modern Athletic White & Crimson Red Design

TREX FIT has been transformed into a **crisp athletic white & crimson red theme** (inspired by high-performance training brands like Nike and Under Armour):
- **Aesthetic**: Clean crisp white background (`#ffffff` / `#f8fafc`), dark charcoal typography (`#0f172a`), vibrant crimson red accents (`#dc2626` / `#b91c1c`), and subtle elevated card drop shadows.
- **Relevant High-Resolution Photography**: Every single exercise, muscle group category, and nutrition meal features relevant, high-resolution fitness and culinary photography.
- **Acoustic Feedback**: Native Web Audio API sound synthesis (crisp UI clicks, 3-note victory chimes, and timer interval countdown beeps).

---

## 🏋️ Features & System Modules

1. **7 Core Muscle Group Hubs:**
   - **Abs & Core, Biceps, Back & Lats, Triceps, Chest & Pecs, Shoulders, Legs & Glutes**
   - **4 Training Regimens per Group**: Home Bodyweight, Gym Hypertrophy, Stretching & Recovery, Dumbbell Only.
   - **Minimum 20 Detailed Exercises per Routine** (over **560+ total exercises**), each with real exercise photography, sets/reps badges, execution guides, and key form cues.
2. **Interactive Interval Workout Rest Timer:**
   - Circular countdown progress ring with color transitions (Red for High Output, Blue for Recovery, Green for Complete).
   - Audio beeps on final 3 countdown seconds and ascending success chimes upon completion.
3. **Precision Nutrition Hub:**
   - **3 Dedicated Regimens**: High-Protein Vegetarian Bulking (2,800 kcal), Lean Non-Veg Shred (2,250 kcal), and Anabolic Keto Carb-Cycling (2,100 kcal).
   - Macro allocations, timed daily meal schedules, ingredient inventories, and step-by-step cooking guides.
4. **30 Science-Backed Health & Recovery Tips:**
   - Peer-reviewed research insights spanning Sleep Hygiene, Hypertrophy Volume Landmarks, Hydration Mechanics, Injury Prehab, and Active Recovery.
5. **Interactive Custom Plan Builder & Todo Checklist:**
   - Custom routine creator with instant checkbox toggles, optimistic UI updates, and satisfying completion audio chimes.
   - Persists custom programs and daily checkoffs directly to the database.
6. **Athlete Profile & Progress Tracker:**
   - Telemetry metrics: completed sessions, active streaks, longest streaks, total calories burned, and total minutes logged.
   - Manual session logger to increment user stats atomically.
   - Honorary achievement badges with locked and unlocked visual states.
